Let the app read your Notion

Notion hands out a password-like key so apps can see the pages you choose. Get yours, paste it here. Has to be done once and takes ~2 minutes.

  1. 1 Open notion.so/my-integrations, press New integration, then copy the secret it shows you.
  2. 2 In Notion, open a page you want to move and choose ⋯ → Connections → your new integration. Pages inside it come too.
The key stays on this computer and is only ever sent to Notion.
